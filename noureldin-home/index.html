Since 1940 we made furniture. Now we make the whole home.

It began as a furniture business with a stubborn love for craft, and for design that outlives trends. Over the decades it grew into a name Egypt trusts, with branches in Cairo and long friendships with architects, interior designers and project teams.

Then the work started to travel. Our pieces now live in projects from Florida to Abidjan, Nairobi, Dubai, Riyadh and Oman. NourEldin Home is the next chapter: all that craft, finally under one roof, planned and delivered to one standard from the first sketch to the last detail.

Not just the years behind us, but the projects that carry our name.
