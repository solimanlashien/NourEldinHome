About Noureldin

Noureldin Home is an extension of Noureldin Furniture, a family-owned business established in 1940, built on decades of experience, craftsmanship, and commitment to quality.

With a strong manufacturing foundation and a comprehensive approach to furnishing, Noureldin Home specializes in delivering integrated furniture and interior finishing solutions for corporate clients, engineering offices, interior designers, developers, and large-scale projects.

Our capabilities cover a wide range of customized products and services, including furniture manufacturing, wall cladding, doors, kitchens, curtains, and complete furnishing solutions — all developed with attention to detail, precise measurements, 3D visualization, and quality standards.

Over the years, our experience has extended across prestigious residential and institutional projects, presidential and ministerial facilities, major residential compounds, foreign government projects, and embassies in Egypt, in addition to international export operations.

We also work as a distributor for multiple established brands, expanding our ability to provide diverse solutions that meet different project requirements.

At Noureldin Home, we bring together heritage, manufacturing expertise, and modern execution to transform concepts into complete spaces.

From concept to completion, we build more than furniture. We build lasting value.
